BRAND STORY REACH US

Jewellery That Lives Beyond The Moment

Rare Heritage was founded in 2014 with a simple but enduring belief: jewellery should be lived in, not locked away. Born from the legacy of Mehta Emporium, the brand has grown into one of India’s most respected fine jewellery houses.

From its 5,000 sq. ft. experiential studio in Mumbai, Rare Heritage has become a destination for women looking not simply for jewellery, but for pieces that feel deeply personal with its creations featured at nearly every marquee Indian wedding.

Steered by Manushi Singh, with the legacy of Varun and Nakshatra Mehta as its foundation, Rare Heritage has built a signature voice in Indian fine jewellery. The brand studies traditional forms not to replicate them, but to reinterpret them for the woman wearing them now. This balance between history and modernity defines the Rare Heritage design language - honouring the depth of tradition while creating pieces that feel entirely relevant to women today.

This philosophy has made Rare Heritage a trusted name among India’s most style-conscious women - recognised by Vogue India, Brides Today, and Bazaar, and present at the country's most celebrated weddings. The brand has expanded internationally, bringing the depth of Indian artisanal craft and tradition to a global stage - with jewellery that is rooted in craft, carried by story, and built to last generations.
