A Rare Heritage Story

— Jewellery That Lives Beyond The Moment

Established in 2014 as an initiative of Mehta Emporium, Rare Heritage has gracefully reshaped the way India approaches bridal jewellery. Born from the legacy of Mehta Emporium, the brand has grown into one of India's most respected fine jewellery houses.

From its 5,000 sq. ft. jewellery atelier in Mumbai, Rare Heritage has become a destination for women looking to embrace pieces that feel deeply personal with its creations featured at nearly every marquee Indian wedding. Every piece is designed for the modern Indian woman - rooted in tradition, refined for the way she lives, moves, and celebrates today.

Steered by Manushi Singh, with the legacy of Nakshatra and Varun Mehta as its foundation, Rare Heritage has built a signature voice in Indian fine jewellery. The brand studies traditional forms not to replicate them, but to reinterpret them for the woman wearing them now. This balance between history and modernity defines the Rare Heritage design language - honouring the depth of tradition while creating pieces that feel entirely relevant to women today.

This philosophy has made Rare Heritage a trusted name among India's most style-conscious women and celebrities - recognised by Vogue India, Brides Today, and Bazaar, and present at the country's most celebrated weddings. The brand is now looking at expanding global horizons - with jewellery rooted in craft, carried by story, and built to last generations.
